Synonyms and Antonyms NVDA Add-on

Introduction

The Synonyms and Antonyms add-on for the NonVisual Desktop Access (NVDA) screen reader is developed by Maria Kausar. It is specifically designed for visually impaired users to make digital reading, vocabulary learning, and study workflows effortless and completely accessible through their screen reader.

Purpose

This add-on serves as an all-in-one vocabulary lookup tool tailored for blind and visually impaired individuals. It supports general book readers looking up words on the fly, as well as candidates preparing for competitive examinations such as PPSC, FPSC, and other academic tests that require quick mastery of synonyms and antonyms.

Key Features

  • Quick synonym and antonym lookup without leaving the active window or reading flow.
  • Designed specifically for the daily needs of visually impaired book readers and competitive exam aspirants.
  • Instant, distraction-free text-to-speech output directly through NVDA.

How to Use

  • Select or highlight the word you want to look up in any document, browser, or reader.
  • Press NVDA + Shift + Y to get the synonyms for the selected word.
  • Press NVDA + Shift + A to get the antonyms for the selected word.
  • NVDA will immediately announce the results so you can continue your reading without interruption.
